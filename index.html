A strategy game for Anki

Every review builds your empire.

Realm Empires turns your daily Anki reviews into a Civilization-style strategy game. Found cities, research technologies, raise wonders and race your friends to victory, using the cards you were going to study anyway.

Download for Anki How it works
Study to play

Your reviews are the game's engine

Every card you answer sends resources to your empire. Cities grow, buildings rise and units move as you study.

📚

Measured against you

Everyone is scored against their own usual workload, so a light reviewer can go head to head with a heavy one.

📱

Phone reviews count

Reviews from AnkiMobile, AnkiDroid or AnkiWeb count as soon as your collection syncs.

⏳

No one needs to be online

Games last one to two weeks for 2 to 6 players. Your empire moves forward whenever you study.

Build a civilization

Cities, districts and wonders on a living map

Every game is a new map with rivers, volcanoes, hidden villages and natural wonders. You decide where every building stands.

Cities

Grow your cities, tile by tile

Citizens work the land around each city. Place Libraries, Markets and Museums on the tiles where they fit, and fill your Archives and Museums with Works of art and writing.

  • Every building shows what it adds before you build it
  • Buy land, improve it with Builders and protect it
Research

A tech tree from Pottery to Chemistry

Nearly 50 technologies, each with a Eureka goal that makes it cheaper. Chemistry unlocks the Research Lab and the Global Clinical Trial, the last step to a Science win.

  • Hover any technology to see what it unlocks
  • Research Labs turn gold into science
Discovery

Natural wonders and hidden villages

Scouts uncover the map. Find a natural wonder like Mount Remembrance and its bonus is yours for the rest of the game.

Choose your path

Three ways to win

Switch strategies at any time. The first player to win any of the three takes the game.

Science

Research Chemistry, then complete the Global Clinical Trial. Libraries, Observatories and Stanford University speed you along.

Renown

Fill your cities with great art and writing, build Monuments and Amphitheaters, and send Envoys on cultural missions.

Frontier

Claim more land than anyone else and keep enough gold to pay its upkeep every night.

The race

Always know where you stand

The Race tab shows every player's progress toward each victory. Build an Observatory, Consulate or Watchtower to see your rivals' numbers.

Espionage

Fight back when rivals pull ahead

Send Spies to sabotage research, steal a Work of art or incite a revolt. Warriors raid land and Outposts push into rival borders.

While you study

Your empire, right on the review screen

A small readout in the corner shows today's progress, what's about to finish and how the race stands, so you can keep studying without opening the game.

Hand-drawn art

Every building and wonder has its own look

From the Library's domed reading room to Stanford University's Hoover Tower and the Sydney Opera House.

Get started

Play your first game in minutes

Install

Download the add-on, then in Anki choose Tools → Add-ons → Install from file and restart.

Practice

Open Tools → Realm Empires (Ctrl+Shift+E, or Cmd+Shift+E on a Mac) and play the short guided Practice game.

Invite friends

Start a real game and share its code, join a public game, or fill the seats with computer players.
